[02] Philosophy — Manifesto v.26

A studio built on sharp edges.

Most websites are assembled. Ours are authored. We start from your commercial goal and work backwards to every typographic and interactive decision, so nothing ships just because a template had a slot for it.

Small by design: four projects a quarter, one senior team from brief to handover, and code you fully own.

Brutal clarity

We remove until only the essential remains: one message per screen, one action per moment, zero decorative noise.
