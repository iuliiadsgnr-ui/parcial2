[W.02] FAQ

Working across borders.

Contracts, payments, time zones and what you actually own at the end. Anything missing? Ask us →

Yes. Most of our clients are in the US, Canada and Europe. We work in English or Spanish, overlap four or more working hours with US time zones and three to five with Europe, and schedule every live call inside that overlap.

We sign a plain-English services agreement before kickoff. Invoices are in USD (EUR on request) and paid through Wise or Stripe. Essential and Boutique are split 50% to start and 50% at handover; Custom projects are invoiced per sprint.

The full source code in a Git repository you own, deployed to your host; the Figma source file; documentation for tokens, components and content editing; and a recorded walkthrough of the codebase. There are no licences or lock-in.

Yes. Static sites can be edited directly in code, or we can connect a lightweight Git-based CMS so your team edits text and images in a simple dashboard. Boutique and Custom include a short training session.

We replace wireframes with the Hero Core Concept: a real, coded first screen you approve before we build the rest. It surfaces the same structural questions as a wireframe, earlier and at full fidelity, so nobody has to imagine the result.

One decision-maker, any existing brand assets, draft content (or the copywriting add-on), and access to your domain and hosting. We send a short checklist the day the contract is signed.

Usually two to four weeks. We take four projects per quarter so every one gets senior attention; the availability panel at the top of this page always shows the next free start date.

Every tier includes post-launch support for bugs and small edits: 14, 30 or 90 days depending on the tier. After that you can add a monthly care plan or book ad-hoc hours.
